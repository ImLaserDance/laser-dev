# Project Vision

## Product Name

Laser.dev

## Personal Brand

LaserDance

## Owner

Miguel Da Silva

## Mission

Build a production-grade personal technology platform that presents Miguel's engineering identity through portfolio work, technical writing, open source projects, newsletter content, and a public AIR reference implementation.

Laser.dev should feel like a serious engineering platform rather than a static resume. It must support publishing, discovery, trust-building, and long-term evolution.

## Audience

- Engineering leaders evaluating technical depth and delivery judgment.
- AI and backend teams looking for architecture perspective.
- Fintech and platform teams looking for pragmatic implementation experience.
- Open source contributors and maintainers.
- Readers who want durable technical writing in English or Spanish.

## Product Pillars

### 1. Professional Identity

Laser.dev is the canonical home for Miguel Da Silva's public technical profile: career narrative, leadership style, domains, projects, talks, writing, and contact paths.

### 2. Technical Authority

The platform should demonstrate backend engineering, architecture, AI systems thinking, delivery leadership, and fintech relevance through concrete artifacts.

### 3. Bilingual Publishing

English and Spanish content must be structurally equal from day one. The platform should not treat Spanish as a later translation layer.

### 4. Open Source Showcase

Projects should present not only code links, but intent, architecture, impact, maturity, and contribution paths.

### 5. AIR Reference Implementation

Laser.dev should model what it teaches: clear metadata, AI-readable pages, stable canonical URLs, structured content, and language-aware machine context.

### 6. Newsletter and Content Hub

The platform should make it easy to publish essays, technical notes, project updates, and recurring newsletter content without coupling content operations to application code.

## Experience Goals

- Fast, accessible, mobile-first interface.
- Clean navigation between languages without losing context.
- Strong SEO and share metadata per language.
- Clear content taxonomy for humans and AI agents.
- Calm, modern visual language aligned with software architecture and delivery leadership.
- Extensible content and metadata model that can support future products, talks, courses, and consulting offers.

## Non-Goals for the Foundation Phase

- No premature CMS lock-in.
- No paid newsletter implementation before the content model is stable.
- No complex animation system before the base information architecture is proven.
- No single-language shortcuts.
